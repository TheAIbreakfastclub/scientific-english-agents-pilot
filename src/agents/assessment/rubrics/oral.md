# Oral Presentation Rubric — Scientific English
# For: 10-minute conference-style presentation
# Level: CEFR B2–C1
# Total: 20 points

---

## Criterion 1 — Content and Scientific Accuracy (5 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 5 | All scientific content accurate and relevant. Claims supported by data or citations. Nothing misleading. |
| 4 | Mostly accurate. One minor imprecision that does not mislead. |
| 3 | Some inaccuracies or unsupported claims. Core argument still valid. |
| 2 | Noticeable errors or gaps that affect the credibility of the talk. |
| 1 | Major scientific errors or very thin content. |
| 0 | Content absent or systematically incorrect. |

---

## Criterion 2 — Structure and Signposting (4 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | Clear IMRAD-aligned structure. Signposting used at all key transitions. Audience always knows where they are in the talk. |
| 3 | Structure clear. 1–2 transitions absent or awkward. |
| 2 | Structure identifiable but signposting inconsistent or absent in places. |
| 1 | Poorly structured. Audience must work to follow the logic. |
| 0 | No discernible structure. |

---

## Criterion 3 — Language Accuracy and Fluency (4 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | Very few errors. Fluent delivery. Occasional hesitation natural and non-disruptive. |
| 3 | Some errors (grammar, vocabulary) but communication not impeded. Mostly fluent. |
| 2 | Frequent errors and/or hesitations that occasionally disrupt comprehension. |
| 1 | Many errors. Listener must make significant effort to understand. |
| 0 | Communication breaks down repeatedly. |

---

## Criterion 4 — Register and Scientific Vocabulary (4 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | Consistently formal. Domain vocabulary precise and accurate. Hedging appropriate to findings. |
| 3 | Mostly appropriate register. 1–2 informal terms or missing hedges. |
| 2 | Some register problems. Imprecise domain vocabulary in places. |
| 1 | Informal register predominates. Domain vocabulary vague or inaccurate. |
| 0 | Register consistently inappropriate for scientific presentation. |

---

## Criterion 5 — Visual Communication (3 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 3 | Slides clear, well-structured, appropriate text density. All figures labelled. Speaker refers to visuals explicitly and naturally. |
| 2 | Slides mostly clear. One or two dense or unlabelled slides. References to visuals present but occasionally awkward. |
| 1 | Slides overloaded or poorly labelled. Speaker rarely connects talk to visuals. |
| 0 | No slides, or slides that impede rather than support comprehension. |
