# Abstract Assessment Rubric
# For: Structured and unstructured scientific abstracts
# Level: CEFR B2–C1
# Total: 10 points

---

## Criterion 1 — Structural Completeness (2 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | All five moves present (Background, Objective, Methods, Results, Conclusion). Word count within ±10% of target. |
| 1 | 3–4 moves present. One move missing or significantly underdeveloped. |
| 0 | Fewer than 3 moves. Structure not recognisable as an abstract. |

---

## Criterion 2 — Information Density (2 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Every sentence carries new, specific information. No redundancy. Key quantitative results included. |
| 1 | Mostly informative but some vague or general statements. Quantitative results absent or imprecise. |
| 0 | Abstract is vague throughout. Could describe almost any study in the field. |

---

## Criterion 3 — Language Accuracy (2 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Virtually error-free. Tenses appropriate to each move. |
| 1 | Some errors (2–5) but none impede comprehension. Minor tense inconsistencies. |
| 0 | Frequent errors impeding comprehension. |

---

## Criterion 4 — Register and Hedging (2 pts)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Fully formal, impersonal. Hedging appropriate for findings (Results/Conclusion). No informal vocabulary. |
| 1 | Mostly appropriate. 1–2 register errors or missing hedges for uncertain findings. |
| 0 | Informal register or systematic misuse of hedging. |

---

## Criterion 5 — Keywords (1 pt)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | 5–8 relevant, domain-specific keywords provided. Not a subset of the title. |
| 0 | Keywords missing, fewer than 5, or not domain-appropriate. |

---

## Criterion 6 — Standalone Clarity (1 pt)

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Abstract can be understood without reading the full paper. All abbreviations defined on first use. |
| 0 | Requires the paper to be understood. Undefined abbreviations present. |
