# IMRAD Writing Rubric
# For: Research article sections (Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion)
# Level: CEFR B2–C1
# Total: 20 points

---

## Criterion 1 — Rhetorical Structure (4 pts)

Assesses whether the student correctly implements the expected moves for the
IMRAD section being evaluated.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | All expected rhetorical moves present and in logical order. Section reads as a coherent, professional scientific text. |
| 3 | Most moves present. One move is underdeveloped or slightly misplaced. |
| 2 | Core structure recognisable but 1–2 moves missing or significantly weak. |
| 1 | Structure incomplete. Reader cannot identify the purpose of the section. |
| 0 | No recognisable IMRAD structure. |

---

## Criterion 2 — Scientific Content Accuracy (4 pts)

Assesses factual correctness, appropriate use of domain knowledge, and
alignment between data and claims.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | All scientific claims are accurate and appropriately supported. No unsupported assertions. Data/evidence used precisely. |
| 3 | Minor inaccuracy or one unsupported claim that does not compromise the overall argument. |
| 2 | Noticeable inaccuracies or missing evidence for key claims. |
| 1 | Significant factual errors or claims made without any supporting evidence. |
| 0 | Scientific content is absent or systematically erroneous. |

---

## Criterion 3 — Language Accuracy (4 pts)

Assesses grammatical correctness, sentence structure, tense consistency,
and absence of L1 interference.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 4 | Very few errors (1–2 minor). No error impedes comprehension. Tenses used correctly throughout. |
| 3 | Some errors (3–5) but none impede comprehension. Tense logic mostly sound. |
| 2 | Frequent errors (6–10) that occasionally impede comprehension. Tense inconsistencies present. |
| 1 | Pervasive errors that often impede comprehension. L1 interference evident. |
| 0 | Text is largely incomprehensible due to language errors. |

---

## Criterion 4 — Register and Style (3 pts)

Assesses appropriateness of academic/scientific register: avoidance of
informal vocabulary, appropriate use of hedging, impersonal constructions.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 3 | Consistently formal, impersonal, hedged where appropriate. No informal vocabulary. Reads as authentic scientific prose. |
| 2 | Mostly appropriate register with occasional informal vocabulary or missing hedges. |
| 1 | Frequent register errors. Informal vocabulary, personal voice where impersonal is required, or systematic over/under-hedging. |
| 0 | Register consistently inappropriate. |

---

## Criterion 5 — Vocabulary Range and Precision (3 pts)

Assesses breadth of academic and domain-specific vocabulary, absence of
vague or repeated vocabulary, appropriate collocations.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 3 | Rich vocabulary appropriate to domain. AWL used accurately. Domain terms precise and consistently applied. No problematic repetition. |
| 2 | Adequate vocabulary. Some AWL used. Occasional vague terms or awkward collocations. |
| 1 | Limited vocabulary. Frequent use of informal or imprecise terms. Collocations often unnatural. |
| 0 | Vocabulary insufficient for scientific communication at this level. |

---

## Criterion 6 — Cohesion and Coherence (2 pts)

Assesses logical flow between sentences and paragraphs, appropriate use of
discourse markers, and clarity of argument progression.

| Score | Descriptor |
|-------|-----------|
| 2 | Ideas linked smoothly. Discourse markers used accurately and varied. Reader can follow the logic without effort. |
| 1 | Some linking present but inconsistent or overused. Occasional jump between ideas. |
| 0 | No visible effort to link ideas. Text reads as disconnected sentences. |
