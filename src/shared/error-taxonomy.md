# Shared: Error Taxonomy — Scientific English
# Used by: language-tutor, assessment agent, writing-coach
# Single source of truth — reference by code (e.g. "Error C2") in all agents.

## Category A — Tense and Aspect
- A1: Wrong tense for IMRAD section
- A2: Inconsistent tense within a section
- A3: Present perfect vs. simple past confusion

## Category B — Voice
- B1: Active where passive conventionally expected (Methods)
- B2: Passive where active is more precise (own contribution, Discussion)
- B3: Passive formed incorrectly ("was demonstrated by us")

## Category C — Hedging
- C1: Missing hedge for uncertain claim
- C2: Overstatement ("proves", "demonstrates conclusively") for non-definitive finding
- C3: Double hedging ("might possibly suggest") — unnecessarily weakens claim
- C4: Wrong hedge strength relative to statistical level

## Category D — Register
- D1: Informal vocabulary (see lexical-expert swap table)
- D2: Personal/emotional language ("I think", "surprisingly", "unfortunately")
- D3: Colloquial connectors ("also" / "but" / "so" at sentence start)

## Category E — Cohesion
- E1: Missing or inappropriate discourse connector
- E2: Pronoun reference unclear
- E3: Paragraph with no topic sentence
- E4: Repeated key noun needing pronoun or synonym substitution

## Category F — Noun Phrase
- F1: Underdeveloped noun phrase ("the cells" → "the HeLa cell line")
- F2: Over-complex noun phrase obscuring meaning
- F3: Missing article before countable noun

## Category G — Citation Integration
- G1: Dropped quotation (no syntactic integration)
- G2: Reference not cited in text
- G3: Citation position incorrect
