The approach

AI as a learning support, not a substitute

Scientific English involves more than linguistic accuracy. It requires interpreting evidence, qualifying claims, explaining methods and communicating uncertainty. This project places these competencies at the centre of AI-assisted feedback.

Competency-based

Connect language development with authentic scientific tasks and learning outcomes.

Formative by design

Help learners identify strengths, diagnose difficulties and plan revisions.

Critical engagement

Encourage justification, reflection and responsible interpretation of evidence.

Teacher-led

Keep pedagogical judgment and assessment decisions with educators.

The system

Six complementary pedagogical agents

The areas below illustrate the six-agent learning experience. Exact agent names and capabilities should be verified against the repository before publication.

01

Scientific writing

Support clarity, structure and disciplinary conventions in scientific texts.

02

Critical reading

Examine claims, evidence, limitations and the strength of conclusions.

03

Academic language

Develop precision, register and appropriate scientific terminology.

04

Scientific communication

Prepare coherent explanations for specialist and non-specialist audiences.

05

Language revision

Reflect on grammar, cohesion, hedging and editorial decisions.

06

Assessment and feedback

Use explicit criteria to guide constructive formative feedback.

Pedagogical foundations

Designed for accountable feedback

The repository describes a six-agent system, three assessment rubrics and support for Claude and Mistral. The pilot aims to preserve these foundations while making them accessible through a browser.

Transparent criteria

Feedback should be traceable to clear pedagogical expectations.

Scientific integrity

AI-generated suggestions require verification and critical scrutiny.

Privacy by design

Never submit identifiable student data to a public demonstration.
